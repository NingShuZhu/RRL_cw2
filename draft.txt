Justification of hyperparameters chosing and their values to search on:

For PPO, the clip coefficient (clip-coef) and the number of update epochs for each collected rollout data (update-epochs) are chosen to tune on.
Reasons: 
clip-coef is an essential hyperparameter in PPO algorithm, clipping policy ratio 是PPO的灵魂所在, 而clip-coef decides how strict the clipping should be. Its value is crucial for the stability and converge speed for the training process.
Values 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 are chosen for grid search because ...

update-epochs decides how much the training exploit the sampled data (i.e., sample efficiency), whose value is crucial for balancing underfitting and overfitting.
Values 5, 10, 15 are chosen for ...
Both of them need to be tuned carefully to achieve stable and efficiency training process and robust and good final model.


For SAC, the target smoothing coefficient for the target network soft updating (tau) and the batch size of sample from the replay buffer (batch-size) are chosen to tune on.
Reasons: 
tau decides the stability of the target for Q-networks' learning, which is crucial for balancing the variance and bias of ... Smaller tau can eliminate variance while bigger tau can reduce the bias. It also decides 训练时的振荡强度.
Values 0.001, 0.005, 0.01 are chosen to test and analyse its influence qualitively.

batch-size can effect exploration强度 (smaller->更强) and optimisation noise (smaller->noisier). Tuning it is important for the exploration-exploitation trade-off and achieve stable training.
Values 64, 256, 512 are chosen because ...


The chosen hyperparameters are tuned on both environments respectively, and the result plots are grouped and shown in the appendix. 
For Ant-v4, as expected, 每个超参数待选值中的中间值取得了最好的表现，in both PPO and SAC. 训练曲线走势与预期一致. 
For PPO, when clip-coef is too small, the return提升 is very slow over the timesteps; when clip-coef is too big, it tends to oscillate around the local optima.
When update-epochs is too small, at the 前半程of training, 提升慢，但后程长势客观，而too large update-epochs则较早停止了return的提升，不利于整体performance。中间值update-epochs有好的学习效率的同时也保障了学习能力。
For SAC, hyperparameters 对于整体性能、稳定性的影响较不直观，表明了SAC算法对不同hyperparameter的robustness. 不过适中的tau和batch-size仍然取得了最好的表现，表明它们能有效trade-off...

For HalfCheetah-v4, the hyperparameter tuning for SAC shows the similar outcome as Ant-v4, the intermediate values of both tau and batch-size achieved the best performance.
However, the hyperparameter tuning for PPO acted abnormal for this environment, where clip-coef=0.1, update-epochs=15 achieved the best performance. It's hard to explane. 
This 进一步shows that PPO is sensitive for different hyperparameters and different environments.


Although the same number of environment steps is assigned to both algorithms, 两算法的内部学习量和对数据的exploit方式不同，跑一样数量的env steps所需的时间也不同。
根据实验，用同样的device，SAC跑400000 env steps大约需要30分钟，而PPO跑1000000 env steps 大约需要15分钟。
所以，In order to fairly compare them, 在最终测试中，SAC的env steps设为400000, 而PPO的设为了2000000, 以求公平的wall clock time.

根据plots可见，给定相同的env steps数，SAC的return增长远远快于PPO，表明了它强大的sample efficiency. 
在近乎相同的wall clock time内，SAC的表现远远超过了PPO。但是SAC的振荡也更严重，有不少大幅振荡，training stability 较差，可能与它的off-policy...性质有关
根据图中的置信区间阴影宽度可见两个算法对于seed的reliability差不多。

可视化结果，描述，
Both algorithms有效学习，取得了较好的return，较好地solve了problems。


由于Half cheetah的PPO grid search结果过于反直觉，值得怀疑这组参数之所以取得好performance是否是因为偶然性。所以除了用这组“best”参数来跑PPO的final run，我又跑了一版用default参数，也就是经验主义下最好的参数，来避免tune时的随机因素干扰导致较差结果。
从plots可见，tune得的best values并非真正的最优参数。而当clip-coef较大时，PPO在Half cheetah任务上的表现非常受随机性影响，poor reliability to seeds。与Ant任务相似，SAC在改任务上的表现远远优于PPO。

qualitative analysis:

对于Ant任务，两个算法都能让ant成功向前但SAC地速度和控制效率明显更好。
